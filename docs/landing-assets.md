# Landing concept photography

The homepage uses three AI-generated concept photographs, not customer testimonials
or video-generation results. The visible captions state that distinction. Images
were created with the built-in ImageGen tool and converted to 900px-wide WebP for
local delivery through Next.js Image. The site does not depend on provider URLs.

- `public/images/home/creator-tumbler.webp`: adult creator presenting an unbranded
  terracotta tumbler in a sunlit kitchen; oatmeal linen shirt, olive background,
  candid expression, warm editorial photography, portrait 2:3, no text or logos.
- `public/images/home/product-tumbler.webp`: the creator image used as a product
  reference; the same tumbler alone on a cream stone counter, diagonal afternoon
  sunlight and shadow, olive background, portrait 2:3, no text or logos.
- `public/images/home/detail-tumbler.webp`: the creator image used as a reference;
  close-up of the tumbler held in hand, collarbone-to-waist crop, natural linen
  texture and warm sunlight, portrait 2:3, no text or logos.

The script choices are explicitly labelled examples. The format control shows a
crop preview of the concept photograph; it does not trigger video generation.
